# Final Project Brief

## Goal
Build an Agentic Workspace in Google Antigravity that automates a real workflow from your work, study, or business context.

## Required Components
- Project brief using SCOPE.
- Workspace map with agents, skills, knowledge, rules, workflow, and checkpoint.
- At least one human checkpoint for risk control.
- At least 3 PDCA loops.
- Output sample from real or approved demo data.
- Presentation checklist.

## Suggested Scope
Pick one workflow that can be explained in 3 minutes and run in 5 minutes.

Good examples:
- CV screening shortlist.
- Customer feedback summary.
- Weekly report preparation.
- Research paper summary.
- Content draft and review workflow.

Avoid:
- Vague all-in-one company automation.
- Sensitive data without anonymization.
- Workflows that require unavailable integrations.

## Submission
Submit the workspace folder and a short demo report.
